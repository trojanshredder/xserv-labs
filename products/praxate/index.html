What it does

You manage the business. Praxate manages the rest.

Your cameras record diligently and tell you almost nothing — until something has already gone wrong. Praxate watches every frame from every camera the moment it arrives, so an incident becomes a message right now, not a footage scrub tomorrow.

Live zone map

Every zone, every person, right now.

Draw zones on any camera feed — reception, workspace, kitchen, server room, pool deck. Praxate tracks who is where and for how long, with identity following each person across cameras.

  • Restricted zones: an unrecognised person near the server room triggers a snapshot, a log entry and an instant alert. Vendor? Tag as guest in two clicks.
  • Break monitoring: who's in the cafeteria, who's at their desk, who's been away longer than allowed. The data speaks — no micromanaging.
  • Zone deviation and dwell anomalies: housekeeping entered Room 204 at 11:40 and nobody has left 35 minutes later. Worth checking.

New · Praxate Bay

1,000+ bays. Multiple tenants. One source of truth.

Parking operations

Every bay, every minute, attributed to a tenant.

Praxate Bay reads every parking space in the building from the cameras you already own, shows each tenant's usage against allocation live, and settles disputes with footage. Built for commercial office complexes where hundreds of cameras already watch the basement — they record, they just don't count.

Each bay is mapped once to the camera that covers it and the tenant it belongs to. Three readings — free, occupied, unreadable — and Bay never counts a space it can't see as free. No number plates read, no drivers identified.

  • Tenant board: allocation vs live usage, flagged the moment anyone goes over
  • WhatsApp alerts with the bay, the time and a clip — encroachment, fire lane blocked, level full
  • Guard board and entry display at the ramp, so arrivals stop circling
  • Monthly utilisation per tenant — reports you can carry into a lease renewal
  • One server in your rack. Your NVR stays the archive; footage stays inside the building

Common questions

What buyers ask on every first call.

What is Praxate?

AI-powered workforce intelligence that runs on the CCTV cameras you already own. It recognises faces for attendance, tracks people across cameras on a live zone map, alerts you about unknown people and restricted zones, and turns the day into analytics and reports — all processed locally on your hardware, with alerts on WhatsApp and the Praxate Companion app.

Do we need to replace our CCTV cameras?

No. Praxate works with any IP camera — CP Plus, Hikvision, Dahua and others — through your existing DVR/NVR or directly over the network. No new cameras, no rewiring. One on-site computer is the entire hardware footprint.

Where is the video processed?

On your premises. Praxate runs on a computer at your site — GPU-accelerated for many cameras, or CPU-only for budget setups. Video never leaves the building; only structured events reach the dashboard and your phone. Face templates are encrypted at rest.

How accurate is the face recognition?

99.7% on well-positioned cameras at decent resolution. Enrollment is a guided, Face-ID-style multi-angle capture, and the detector keeps improving on your own footage — hard frames are collected and the model retrained on site. We survey every site first and tell you which cameras to move before anyone is enrolled.

How much does Praxate cost?

Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial and no credit card. Starter ₹4,999/month (up to 4 cameras), Growth ₹9,999 (up to 8), Professional ₹14,999 (up to 12, with the Companion app), Enterprise ₹19,999 (up to 16, REST API, multi-location). Above 16 cameras and for Praxate Bay we quote per site. Unlimited employees on every plan.

How long does setup take?

We set it up for you — on-site in Hyderabad, remote elsewhere. Typical go-live is seven to fourteen days from install: a pilot in one wing or function, alerts validated with your team, then property-wide.

Is face-based attendance legal in India?

Yes, when employees are notified and consent is documented — which Praxate has workflows for. Guest faces are only ever matched against opt-in VIP lists. Because processing is on-prem, the consent conversation is short. We help you draft signage and consent.

What is Praxate Bay?

The same engine pointed at parking. Each bay is mapped once to the camera that covers it and the tenant it belongs to. Bay reads free, occupied or unreadable every minute, shows usage against allocation live, alerts on encroachment and blocked fire lanes on WhatsApp with a clip, and produces monthly utilisation per tenant. No plates read, no drivers identified. Built for office complexes with hundreds of cameras and 1,000+ bays.
