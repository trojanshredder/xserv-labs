What it is

One app for everything you run today.

You already know what slows your day down: "Where is the truck?", Excel that nobody updates, diesel that doesn't add up, drivers who've been on the road 14 hours. Traxium handles each one without you having to do anything different. No new hardware — it works with the GPS devices you already have.

The dashboard

Good morning. Here's your fleet.

Active trucks, what's in transit, what's been idle too long, revenue month-to-date. Every trip with vehicle, route, driver, live status and ETA — on one screen, on your phone or the office computer.

  • Built for India — dhaba breaks, toll plazas, RTO checkpoints and state borders handled correctly, not flagged as problems.
  • Hindi works. The whole app, and the assistant.
  • Nothing to install. Works in any browser.
Customer share

One link. Customers track their own truck.

Every trip has a public web link. No app to install, no login, works on any phone. The customer sees the truck moving, the route, and simple stop labels — "Lunch break", "Toll", "Rest stop". No raw speeds or scary delay numbers.

A 90-minute halt at a dhaba shows up as "Driver rest stop". Your customer reads it and moves on. You don't have to explain anything.

Alerts

WhatsApp messages when something matters.

If a truck stops for too long, you get a message. Not for every traffic light — only for stops that actually mean something.

  • 10 min — first nudge · 30 min — worth checking · 60 min — looks like a real halt
  • +30 min — keeps you posted · moves — one final message
  • Also: arrival at destination, route deviation, low fuel, document expiry.
Analytics

See what each customer pays you, every week.

Week-by-week breakdown for each customer — Amazon, Flipkart, market loads, empty trips. Freight earned, diesel cost, AdBlue, repairs and tolls, driver wages → net per trip.

One operator found out his "best" customer was actually losing him ₹3,200 per trip once diesel and tolls were counted. He renegotiated the next week.

Trips, drivers, paperwork

You create the trip. Status updates on its own.

Truck enters the loading point → status becomes Loading. Leaves → In transit. Reaches destination → Reached. All automatic, using 200 m GPS geofences around origin and destination. Add planned mid-route stops, reassign cargo to a backup vehicle if the primary breaks down — the customer's link stays the same.

Common questions

The ones we get on every demo call.

What is Traxium?

One app for everything a transporter runs today — GPS, trip sheets, fuel logs, invoices, driver attendance and customer share links. Trucks show up on one map, trips update their own status through geofences, customers get a live link instead of calling you, and you get a WhatsApp message only when something actually needs attention.

Who is Traxium built for?

Indian fleet owners and transporters — from 5 trucks to a few hundred — who currently run the business on phone calls, Excel and WhatsApp groups. It's built for India, not borrowed from foreign software: dhaba breaks, toll plazas, RTO checkpoints and state borders are handled correctly, and it works in Hindi.

Does Traxium work with my existing GPS devices?

Yes. No new hardware — Traxium works with the GPS units already fitted in your trucks, and we help you connect them the same day. Positions refresh every 30 seconds.

How much does Traxium cost?

30-day free trial with unlimited vehicles and no card. Then Starter at ₹2,500/month for up to 10 vehicles, Pro at ₹10,000/month for up to 50 vehicles (adds driver safety, tolls, HR and the assistant), and Enterprise on request with unlimited vehicles, API access and an account manager. Billed monthly, cancel anytime, GST extra.

How do customers track their own truck?

Every trip gets a public web link. No app, no login, any phone. They see the truck moving, the route, and simple stop labels — "Lunch break", "Toll", "Driver rest stop". No raw speeds or scary delay numbers, so you don't have to explain anything.

What alerts does Traxium send on WhatsApp?

Halts that actually mean something — a first nudge at 10 minutes, worth checking at 30, a real halt at 60, an update every 30 after that, and one final message when the truck moves. Also arrival at destination, route deviation, low fuel, driver fatigue, and document expiry at 60, 30, 7 and 1 day before.

Is Traxium GST compliant?

Yes. A completed trip becomes an invoice in two clicks with GST applied, and payments are tracked against each invoice. Every kilometre, stop and halt is timestamped — which is what ends billing disputes.

Who owns my data?

You do. Encrypted at rest, backed up daily, never shared with anyone else. Exportable any time.
