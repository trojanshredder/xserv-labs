Common questions

The ones we get on every demo call.

What is Traxium?

The all-in-one TMS for Indian fleets: state-of-the-art tracking, complete operations management, and GST-ready invoicing — from geofence to cash, in one system. Tuned for dhaba breaks, toll plazas and Amazon SLAs. 2,300+ vehicles tracked daily at 99.9% uptime.

Who is Traxium built for?

Fleets that deliver to India's biggest — Amazon, Flipkart, BlueDart, Delhivery, Ecom Express, DTDC, Shadowfax. Transporters manage every trip, document and invoice in one place, mapped to the client's SLA. Enterprises get real-time visibility across every vendor's fleet without building anything.

Does Traxium work with my existing GPS devices?

Yes. The live map refreshes every 30 seconds across WheelsEye and Veyron. Sign up, paste your WheelsEye token, and every vehicle is imported automatically. No installation, no hardware, no training.

How much does Traxium cost?

First month free, no card. Standard: ₹2,500/month for up to 10 vehicles, ₹5,000 for up to 25, ₹10,000 for up to 50, ₹15,000 for up to 75, from ₹20,000 for 100+. Pro + AI & Analytics: ₹7,500 for up to 25, ₹12,500 for up to 50, ₹17,500 for up to 75. Plus GST, billed monthly, cancel anytime.

Is Traxium GST compliant?

Yes. Cleared trips become GST-compliant GTA invoices with the right rates out of the box — 5% RCM, 5% no-ITC or 18% FCM, never a wrong 12%. A finance MIS with two-stage Ops → Finance clearance feeds driver settlements, ICICI-format payout files and Tally vouchers. Fill-template invoicing renders your customer's own .xlsx straight to PDF.

How do customers track their own truck?

Every trip gets a public share page that shows a clean route with smart stop labels — "Lunch break", "Toll", "Driver rest stop" — never a raw speed. No app, no login.

Does anything auto-close?

No. One hard rule: nothing auto-closes. Geofences auto-promote status at pickup and drop, but a human always confirms the trip is done.
